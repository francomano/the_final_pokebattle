Project: The Final PokéBattle (Full MMO RPG Online)

Overview
- This document gathers the project concept, design notes, and technical decisions.
- The project is a fully online MMO RPG: there is no preparation phase. Players drop
  directly into the adventure map and spawn into the game world.

Core Concept
- The server stores and manages all data needed during a match. When the adventure
  ends, the match data is deleted and only the final result is kept: the winner.
- There is no lobby and no pre-game setup: a match IS an entire adventure played out
  inside the map. During the adventure players explore, battle wild creatures,
  collect items and interact with the world.
- The final battle is the showdown between the players (PvP).

Adventure & Final Battle Flow
- The adventure lasts at most 3 minutes.
- The final battle starts when either:
  - a player decides to reach the final area, or
  - the adventure time limit expires.
- Players arrive at the final showdown with the conditions and resources they
  accumulated during the adventure.
- The battle uses the game state exactly as it is at the moment the battle starts.
- When the battle ends, the winner is determined.
- The server conserves only the final result: the winner.

Online Mode
- The server handles the chat and the loading of clues (hints) between players.
- Match data is scrapped after the match; only the winner is recorded.

Offline Mode (current focus)
- A single-player practice mode that mirrors the online rules.
- The AI opponent moves differently every match, behaving like a human player,
  and the 3-minute limit is kept.
- The same map type can be replayed many times, but every match plays out
  differently (new AI behaviour, shuffled events).
- New characters (new playable character + starter) can be unlocked by defeating
  them. For example: Blue is removed from the playable roster and is always the
  rival in the forest map; defeating him unlocks him as playable.
- Signs hold static clues on the map (e.g. where a rare creature can be found).
- NPC dialogues change (giving suggestions/hints) depending on what the opponent
  is doing.
- Like online: reaching the central area and standing on a specific spot triggers
  the final challenge; otherwise it triggers when the 3 minutes expire.

Technical Goals
- Runtime asset generation: all sprites are extracted from a data source (GBA ROM)
  at runtime — no pre-baked assets in the repository.
- Deterministic-ish maps driven by data files (JSON) so layouts, portals, NPC
  placement, encounter zones and clue content can evolve per match.
- Focus areas needed for the new vision:
  - a playing AI opponent (human-like, varied each match)
  - an adventure timer (3 minutes) driving the final-battle trigger
  - a final-area trigger point
  - character unlock (defeat rival -> unlock playable)
  - dynamic NPC dialogue driven by opponent state
  - (online, later) a server handling chat + clue delivery and pruning match data

Repository layout (offline prototype currently)
- src/game.py — GameState, movement, battles, items, surf, timers
- src/frontend.py — Pygame rendering, input, camera, animations
- src/rom_reader.py — Reads creature data (stats, types, moves) from ROM
- src/sprite_extractor.py — Extracts and caches visual assets from ROM
- data/maps.json — Map layouts, portals, NPCs, tiles, clue zones
- data/characters.json — Playable characters and rival configuration

Current concrete progress
- Offline prototype with character select, exploration, wild encounters, HM Cut
  and HM Surf (with real ROM overworld surf sprites and mount/dismount animation).
- Surfing mounts the Pokémon when entering water and dismounts when returning to land.

Not yet implemented (per current direction)
- The human-like AI opponent.
- The 3-minute adventure timer + central-area/spot final-battle trigger.
- Character unlocking by defeating rivals.
- Dynamic NPC dialogue driven by the opponent.
- The online server (chat, clue loading, match-data pruning).
